Step 3 – Are you currently enrolled on the Nissan Australia mainframe (NISCOM)?
If no, please speak to your Manager or colleague and ask them to enrol you. If yes, your mainframe (NISCOM) registration number is your lifetime Ambassador number AND your Student Number whilst with Nissan.

Final check list:

• Enrolled on the Nissan Mainframe and holder of Ambassador/Student number (step 3)

2. How do I register for Ambassador Club?

Step 4 - You will receive a welcome letter from the Nissan Ambassador Club Team within the next 4-6 weeks.
The letter will contain the following:
• Confirmation of your details entered on Nissan mainframe (NISCOM)
• Login information for the AC17 website
• Access monthly statements via the dealernet
• Access our member guide
• How to start earning credits
• How to become an eligible registered member – You must register once every AC year to be eligible for rewards and awards – it will only take a minute of your time
We look forwarding to working with you in 2017.

Go to www.nissanevents.com.au/ac2017/reg and successfully complete the 2017 Nissan Ambassador Club registration form including a requirement to accept the program terms and conditions as directed. On completion, you will receive a confirmation e-Mail for your Nissan Ambassador Club registration.
