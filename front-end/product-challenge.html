PRODUCT CHALLENGE

Who will be the 2017 Masters Champion……….

The Nissan Product Challenge Events are a game of skill and knowledge.

The events have been running for over 15 years and are attended annually by over 1000 Nissan dealership employees and Nissan Australia staff. There are 7 events as detailed below.

Each dealership is invited to their respective State’s event and can bring along a team of up to 12 staff members. Representation across every department is encouraged.

Teams will compete for the title of 2017 State Product Challenge Champions and the recently introduced National Masters Champion.

There are 3 rounds of questions throughout the evening. Each round consists of 15 questions ranging from Product Knowledge, Finance, Training, Parts, Service and even Ambassador Club rules and guidelines. Maximum points per round is 25 unless an Ace card is used which will double the score for that round. (1 Ace card per dealership, can be used once only during the event)

First prize, for each of the seven 2017 State Product Challenge Champions, is a superb Product Challenge perpetual trophy and $2000 in Ambassador Club Reward cards, to be divided within the winning dealership’s team. The Runner-up receives $800 in Ambassador Club Reward cards.

The National Masters Champion is awarded to the dealership with the highest score nationally at the completion of the 7 events. Currently held by Motors Nissan Launceston, the winner will receive a magnificent perpetual trophy and $2000 in Ambassador Club reward cards to go towards a dealership celebratory lunch or dinner.

An important part of the Product Challenge events is the Rewards and Recognition section of the evening. Here we get to present Loyalty Awards to members in front of their peers. Very proud moments for each of the Ambassador Club high achieving members.

Check below for the date and venue for your State’s event and start studying now.
