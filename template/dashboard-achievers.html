High Achiever Award


An exclusive invitation to join our Managing Director’s Guild Event is now extended to our High Achievers. Introduced in the AC16 program this is now an annual award. The criteria for achieving High Achiever Status demands excellence in every area of your job description. There are no minimum lifetime credits to earn but much to experience in meeting our esteemed Guild members along with your peers and partner in recognition of your outstanding achievement. If you are ranked in one of the places listed in the table below at 31 March in the current AC program your place is secured.
