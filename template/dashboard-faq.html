Frequently Asked Questions

How and when do I have to register?

Members must register once every program year 1 April – 31 March at www.nissan-events.com.au/ac2017/reg

How do I join Ambassador Club?

Refer to “Are you eligible?” on the login page.  You must be enrolled on the Nissan mainframe to hold a 5digit student/Ambassador number and be in an Ambassador Club eligible role.  You will be automatically rolled over into Nissan Ambassador Club, receiving a Welcome Pack with your details, and notification for your compulsory registration at  www.nissan-events.com.au/ac2017/reg.  On completion you will receive an e-Mail confirmation and only then be eligible to earn Awards/Rewards within Nissan Ambassador Club.

Am I eligible to participate in Sprint Incentives?

Yes, providing the criteria is applicable to your role and all AC & Sprint Incentive T&Cs are met.  Nissan Ambassador Club registration is compulsory for all incentives.

Am I eligible in Ambassador Club if I am enrolled on the Nissan mainframe?

If you are enrolled on the Nissan mainframe in an eligible AC role you are a member of AC but NOT an eligible member until you have registered in the current program period. Every member must register once in every AC program period (1 April – 31 March) to be eligible.

What is the Ambassador Club program period?

1 April – 31 March annually

Where can I find my metrics and criteria for Ambassador Club?

Go to the Member Guide tab and click your department/role tab. You will find all information about Ambassador Club here.
