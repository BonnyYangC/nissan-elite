NISSAN DEALER EXCELLENCE PROGRAM

Nissan is pleased to launch the FY16 Dealer Excellence Program which runs from 1st April 2016 to 31st March 2017. A summary of the mechanics and criteria are outlined in this bulletin.

The Nissan Dealer Excellence Program recognises and rewards true sustained excellence in key business aspects that ensure sound growth and profitability for your dealership and Nissan. These key business aspects include:

  • Sales for new vehicles and after sales,
  • Excellent customer experience
  • Training and product knowledge
  • NFSA finance penetration
  • Nissan system compliance practices
  • Dealership Standards on Facilities & Parts/Service

As well as significant rewards and recognition, those dealerships who attain Dealer Excellence status will be able to market themselves in their respective PMA and communities as such for 12 months.
