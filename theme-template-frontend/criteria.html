QUALIFYING CRITERIA

To be eligible for the FY16 Dealer Excellence Program and any rewards, a Dealership must achieve the minimum standard requirement for the program period as outlined below.


MINIMUM STANDARD for Dealer Excellence program eligibility
RSE TARGET Must meet 100% RSE Target for last 12 months rolling average (April 2016 to March 2017)

 

plus MINIMUM STANDARD for Nissan Global Award eligibility
CE SALES & SERVICE RECOMMENDATION TARGETS Must meet or exceed both CE Targets for Sales and Service recommendation for the last 6 months rolling average (October 2016 to March 2017)


Scoring Criteria

If qualified by March 2016, awards are based on the following monthly point system.  Points are earned each month covering key aspects of the business. 

Points are awarded monthly across key business aspects with top Dealerships ranked to determine awards and recognition. Dealerships can earn as many points as possible throughout the program period.
