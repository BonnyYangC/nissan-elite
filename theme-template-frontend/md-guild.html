The Managing Director’s Guild is an elite club reserved for those who have reached 300,000 credits throughout their total Ambassador Club membership period, (lifetime credits).

Upon reaching 325,000 credits, (Gold status), members will qualify for induction into the Nissan Ambassador Club Managing Director’s Guild.

Induction includes an invitation for both the member and their partner to an induction event hosted by the Nissan Australia Managing Director.

Induction events are held annually to honour new inductees. If there are no inductees in any given Ambassador Club program year the Guild will not meet.

Once inducted into the Managing Director’s Guild you become a Foundation member.  (MDG Gold 300k credits, MDG Platinum 500k credits)

To be invited to future induction events you must either maintain Ambassador status within each program year, (refer to the member guide for position requirements) or Ambassador Status Average. (lifetime credits/years of service)

MD Guild members are our most valued members of the Club and have been fortunate to travel to Induction locations such as Sydney, Melbourne, Barossa Valley, Uluru and for the 2017 event, Hamilton Island.
